# Manjira Tracker — progress report

Date: 13 October 2026

## Completed task-list work

- The replacement photographic Vitthal idol asset is used in lessons and rhythm practice.
- Tardi demonstrations show fingers gripping the metal rims to damp vibration. The lesson introduction now describes that grip rather than pressing the instruments together quickly.
- User-facing lesson titles, demonstration labels, practice cards, journey summaries, rhythm beat cards, and waveform diagnostics use Murda for open strikes and Tardi for closed strikes. Calibration and live studio already use the same terminology.
- The guided lesson journey now teaches Tardi, then Murda, then Tardi–Murda alternation, followed by ear training. Alternation sound demonstrations also play Tardi before Murda.
- Basic four-beat, alternate-beat, and Keherwa exercises now follow the Tardi-first convention in the lesson data. The seven-beat Roopak exercise remains Tardi-first. These are the application's exercise arrangements; musical correctness still needs practitioner validation.
- The original lesson and rhythm layouts, Vitthal backgrounds, animations, beat cards, result screens, and fullscreen controls are retained.

## USB diagnostic check

At the time of inspection, Linux reported no /dev/ttyUSB* or /dev/ttyACM* ports and no external USB audio interface. Only built-in audio cards were listed. The user identified the device as a transducer connected through a USB audio interface/sound card. A USB audio mixer should normally be selected under Microphone → Audio input; USB serial is for serial telemetry devices. The app now refreshes audio inputs when devices are plugged in or unplugged and explains the distinction between USB audio and USB serial. Hardware detection, cable, and interface identity still need checking.
